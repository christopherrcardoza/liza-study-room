Head tracking

Your webcam moves the view when you move your head. Nothing is recorded and nothing leaves this machine.
H turns Look Around on and off. Shift+H re-centres your head. Pick Turn my head above and allow the camera when the browser asks.
What this build does and does not do. It plays a real proceeding at the speed you choose, in a room you can look around, and gives you somewhere to write. It does not score you and it never opens your microphone — there is no speech recognition in this build at all. Check your own work against the official transcript, which the Finish button reveals. If a button you expected is missing, this is why, and it is deliberate rather than broken.
